Choosing an agency

What to Check Before You Bring On a Staffing Agency

Whichever agency you use, confirm four things in writing. First, that every clinician is licensed for the role and state. Second, that background and drug screening are completed before the first shift. Third, that the agency checks federal exclusion lists. Fourth, who is the employer of record and who carries workers' compensation.

The exclusion check matters because, according to the HHS Office of Inspector General exclusions FAQ, no Federal health care program pays for items or services furnished, ordered or prescribed by an excluded person, and that applies to anyone who employs or contracts with the excluded person. The OIG updates its List of Excluded Individuals/Entities (LEIE) generally by the middle of each month. Hearthstone runs monthly OIG and SAM exclusion checks as part of credentialing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions From Houston Facility Partners

How fast can Hearthstone place a clinician at my facility?

We aim to fill any role within 36-72 hours after a facility requests a shift. That is a placement target, not a guarantee. New facilities start with the Request Staffing form, an email to facilities@hearthstoneandco.com, or a call to (713) 938-3989.

Are your clinicians W-2 employees or 1099 contractors?

Hearthstone clinicians are W-2 employees, and W-2 clinicians are covered by Hearthstone's workers' compensation. Hearthstone handles payroll and employment obligations; Texas employer information is available from the Texas Workforce Commission. See our guide to W-2 vs 1099 staffing for facilities.

Which roles do you staff?

Our core is RNs, LVNs and CNAs: RN staffing, LVN staffing and CNA staffing. Hearthstone also hires and staffs allied healthcare and other medical roles. Tell us the role in your request and we will confirm availability.

Which areas of Greater Houston do you serve?

We serve Harris, Fort Bend, Montgomery and Brazoria Counties, including Houston, Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands, Pearland, Missouri City, Spring, Cypress, Conroe and surrounding communities.

What types of facilities do you staff?

We staff skilled nursing facilities (SNFs), assisted living and memory care communities, hospitals and acute-care units, behavioral health and psychiatric facilities, home health agencies, outpatient clinics, rehabilitation centers and dialysis clinics across Greater Houston.

What does your credentialing process include?

Credentialing is managed by Hearthstone and follows five steps. Background screening runs through Checkr and FACIS Level 3, drug screening is through Quest, and we run monthly OIG and SAM exclusion checks. See compliance and credentialing for more.

What contract options are available?

Standard contracts run 13 weeks, and per diem coverage is available through our per diem program. Activation takes about 1-2 weeks, and Hearthstone manages full credentialing. Exact terms are confirmed with your facilities contact; contact facilities@hearthstoneandco.com to discuss your needs.

Do you offer emergency or urgent coverage?

We offer a 48-hour emergency response SLA for travel nurse staffing; see travel nurse staffing for details. For urgent shift needs, call (713) 938-3989 or submit a request and we follow up on every one.

How do I start a staffing request?

Use the Request Staffing form with your facility, roles, shifts and dates, or book a 20-minute call. You can also email facilities@hearthstoneandco.com.
