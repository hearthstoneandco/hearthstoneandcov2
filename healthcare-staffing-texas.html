Texas Healthcare Staffing

Healthcare Staffing in Texas: RN, LVN and CNA

Hearthstone & Co. Workforce Solutions is a veteran-owned and woman-owned healthcare staffing company based in Katy, TX, serving Greater Houston and Texas. Our core is W-2 RNs, LVNs and CNAs, with credentialing managed by Hearthstone.

Request Staffing Call (713) 938-3989

Who We Staff For

Hearthstone supplies RNs, LVNs and CNAs to healthcare facilities in Greater Houston and Texas. We also hire and staff allied healthcare and other medical roles.

We serve skilled nursing facilities, assisted living communities, home health agencies, hospitals, behavioral health facilities and clinics, on per diem, contract and temp-to-perm terms, plus travel assignments.

Headquartered in Katy

Hearthstone is headquartered at 29655 Jordan Crossing Blvd, Katy, TX 77494. Our home market is Greater Houston, including Harris, Fort Bend and Montgomery counties and Pearland. See our pages for Houston, Katy, Sugar Land, Cypress and The Woodlands.

We do not operate offices outside Katy. For facilities outside Greater Houston, tell us the location, role and shift needs, and we will confirm timing and logistics with you before a shift is booked.

Texas Credentialing Basics

Texas nurse licensure is administered by the Texas Board of Nursing, which explains how LVN and RN licenses are verified. Nurse aides are listed on the Texas Nurse Aide Registry kept by HHSC; see the Texas Nurse Aide Registry rules (26 Tex. Admin. Code §556.9). For any role paid through federal health care programs, the HHS Office of Inspector General explains why an excluded person cannot provide services.

At Hearthstone, credentialing is managed by us: Checkr and FACIS Level 3 background screening, Quest drug screening, five-step credentialing and monthly OIG and SAM exclusion checks. Clinicians are W-2 employees paid weekly and covered by Hearthstone workers’ compensation. See compliance and credentialing.

Veteran-Owned and Woman-Owned

Hearthstone is SBA-certified as a Veteran-Owned Small Business (VOSB) and Woman-Owned Small Business (WOSB), and is a NaVOBA-certified Veteran’s Business Enterprise. Our founder, Renaye Mullings, is a U.S. Army veteran. Details are on our certifications page, and our work with prime contractors is on the government contracting page.

How to Request Staff
