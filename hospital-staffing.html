What Is Hospital Staffing

Acute Care Coverage That Keeps Your Floors Running

Hospital staffing means placing nurses and clinical staff who can step onto an acute care floor and perform from the first shift — at the pace, acuity, and documentation standard that hospital environments demand.

Hospitals run on tight ratios and unforgiving timelines. A single call-out on a telemetry floor or an unexpected ICU admission can put both patient safety and staff morale at risk. Hearthstone maintains a Houston-area network of acute-care-experienced clinicians who are credentialed and ready, so you can cover gaps without pulling exhausted staff into mandatory overtime.

We support med-surg, telemetry, step-down, emergency, and critical care units across Harris, Fort Bend, and Montgomery Counties — with per diem coverage for short-notice gaps and contract staffing for sustained needs during census surges, seasonal volume, or extended leaves. Every clinician is screened for the specific unit and acuity level before they are matched to your hospital.

How Hospital Placement Works

Facilities with an active Hearthstone staffing agreement can request acute care coverage through a straightforward, ratio-aware process.

  • Submit your open shift — unit, acuity, ratio, and skill level needed
  • We match an acute-care-experienced clinician from our network
  • Unit-specific competencies and certifications confirmed in advance
  • Credentialing packet provided before the shift begins
  • Clinician arrives briefed on your unit protocols and charting system
  • Timesheet and invoice documented after shift completion

FAQ

Hospital Staffing — Common Questions

Which hospital units can Hearthstone staff?

We place clinicians across med-surg, telemetry and step-down, emergency, and ICU/critical care units. Every placement is matched to the specific unit and acuity level you request, so the clinician arrives with relevant experience.

How quickly can you provide hospital coverage?

For facilities with an active staffing agreement, same-week coverage is standard and same-day is possible for many roles depending on availability. The right unit match always takes priority over speed.

Do you offer contract and block-booked coverage, not just per diem?

Yes. We provide per diem coverage for short-notice gaps and contract or block-booked staffing for sustained needs such as census surges, seasonal volume, and extended leaves. We'll help you choose the model that fits the situation.

Are your clinicians credentialed for acute care?

Every clinician is pre-credentialed — active Texas licensure, background and OIG/SAM screening, BLS and any specialty certifications such as ACLS, and current health and immunization records — verified before the first shift. Specialty competencies are confirmed for the unit you're staffing.

What documentation do we receive for each placement?

Before each placement you receive a credentialing summary for the assigned clinician confirming licensure, screening clearance, and certifications. A signed timesheet and invoice follow after each shift.
