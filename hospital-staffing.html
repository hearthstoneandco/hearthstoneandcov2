What Is Hospital Staffing

Acute Care Coverage That Keeps Your Floors Running

Hospital staffing means placing nurses and clinical staff from an outside agency to cover open shifts on an acute care floor while your permanent team stays within safe ratios.

Hospitals run on tight ratios and unforgiving timelines. A single call-out on a telemetry floor or an unexpected admission can put patient safety and staff morale at risk. Hearthstone supplies W-2 RNs, LVNs and CNAs with credentialing managed by Hearthstone, so you can cover gaps without pulling exhausted staff into overtime. We also staff allied health and other medical roles.

We serve hospital and clinical teams across Harris, Fort Bend, and Montgomery Counties, with per diem coverage for short-notice gaps and contract staffing for sustained needs such as census surges, seasonal volume, or extended leaves.

Know the Rules

What Hospitals Should Know Before Using Agency Nurses

24-hour RN-supervised nursing. The CMS hospital Condition of Participation for nursing services, 42 CFR 482.23, requires hospitals to provide 24-hour nursing services furnished or supervised by a registered nurse, to have a licensed practical (vocational) nurse or registered nurse on duty at all times, and to maintain adequate numbers of licensed nurses and other personnel to care for all patients as needed.

Agency nurses follow your policies. The same regulation states that licensed nurses who provide services in the hospital must adhere to the hospital's policies and procedures, whether they are employees or contract, leased or other non-employee staff. Build unit orientation, charting-system access and policy review into every agency placement.

Our screening. Credentialing is managed by Hearthstone through a five-step process, with background screening through Checkr and FACIS Level 3, Quest drug screening, and monthly OIG and SAM exclusion checks. Ask any agency for the same detail. See our compliance and credentialing page and the staffing agency vetting scorecard.

FAQ

Hospital Staffing — Common Questions

What is hospital staffing?

Hospital staffing is the placement of nurses and other clinical staff from an outside agency to cover open shifts, call-outs, leaves and census surges on hospital units. Hearthstone & Co. primarily staffs RNs, LVNs and CNAs as W-2 employees across Greater Houston, with allied and other medical staffing available. We respond to every request within 24-48 hours, and our goal is to fill the role within 36-72 hours of the initial request.

Which hospital roles does Hearthstone staff?

Our core is RNs, LVNs and CNAs. We also hire and staff allied healthcare and other types of medical staffing, so tell us the role you need on the request form. Unit and specialty requirements are reviewed per request; we confirm what we can cover before a clinician is placed.

How quickly can you provide hospital coverage?

We respond to every facility staffing request within 24-48 hours to begin reviewing it. Our goal is to fill the role within 36-72 hours after the initial request. That is a placement target, not a guarantee, and depends on the role, shift and unit requirements.

Do you offer contract and block-booked coverage, not just per diem?

Yes. We support per diem coverage for short-notice gaps and contract staffing for sustained needs such as census surges, seasonal volume and extended leaves. Our standard contract is 13 weeks, and we can help you choose the model that fits the situation.

How are your clinicians screened and credentialed?

Credentialing is managed by Hearthstone through a five-step process. Background screening uses Checkr and FACIS Level 3, drug screening is through Quest, and we run monthly OIG and SAM exclusion checks. Ask for the specific documents you need for your facility's credentialing file.

Do agency nurses have to follow our hospital's policies?

Yes. Under the CMS hospital Condition of Participation for nursing services (42 CFR 482.23), licensed nurses who provide services in the hospital must adhere to the hospital's policies and procedures, regardless of whether they are employees, contract, leased or other non-employee staff. Plan orientation to your unit protocols and charting system for every agency placement.

What does CMS require for hospital nursing coverage?

42 CFR 482.23 requires 24-hour nursing services furnished or supervised by a registered nurse, with a licensed practical (vocational) nurse or registered nurse on duty at all times, and adequate numbers of licensed nurses and other personnel to care for all patients as needed. Agency staffing can help you meet those requirements when permanent staff are short.

Are your clinicians W-2 employees?

Yes. Hearthstone clinicians are W-2 employees, covered by Hearthstone's workers' compensation, and paid weekly.

What documentation do we receive for each placement?

Tell us what your facility's credentialing file requires, such as license verification, screening results and certifications, and we will confirm what we can provide before the shift. A timesheet and invoice follow each shift.

How do I request hospital staff?

Use the Request Staffing form with your unit, shift dates and the role you need, or call (713) 938-3989. A Hearthstone team member will respond within 24-48 hours.
