Pay & Requirements

What LVNs Earn in Houston

LVN pay in Greater Houston typically runs $27–$38 per hour, depending on shift, facility type, and experience. Weekend, night, and specialty-unit shifts generally land at the higher end of that range. Every Hearthstone LVN is a W-2 employee — not a 1099 contractor — which means weekly pay and payroll tax withholding, not just a per-shift check.

LVN Jobs in Houston — FAQs

How much do LVNs make in Houston?
LVN pay in Greater Houston typically ranges from $27 to $38 per hour, depending on shift, facility, and experience level. Weekend, overnight, and specialty-unit shifts usually pay toward the higher end.
Do I need experience to apply?
Recent hands-on LVN experience is preferred, but we consider new-grad LVNs with an active Texas license on a case-by-case basis.
Am I a W-2 employee or a 1099 contractor?
Every LVN placed through Hearthstone is a W-2 employee. That means weekly pay and payroll tax withholding, not just a per-shift 1099 payment. After your first 90 days, you become eligible for the same benefits as our internal team: paid time off, paid holidays, group medical, dental and vision coverage with a company contribution, and a 401(k) plan.
Which cities and counties do you cover?
We place LVNs across Harris, Fort Bend, and Montgomery counties, including Houston, Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands, Pearland, Missouri City, Conroe, Spring, and Cypress.
How fast can I start working?
Most LVNs complete the application in under five minutes. Once your license and BLS are verified, we begin matching you to open shifts right away.
