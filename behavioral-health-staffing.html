What Is Behavioral Health Staffing

Specialized Coverage for Psychiatric and Mental Health Settings

Behavioral health staffing places clinicians who are specifically trained for psychiatric, mental health, and substance-use environments — settings where clinical skill, patient safety, and de-escalation experience matter just as much as licensure.

Staffing a behavioral health unit is not the same as filling a med-surg shift. The clinicians who succeed in these environments are those who can manage acute psychiatric presentations, maintain therapeutic boundaries, document under heightened regulatory scrutiny, and respond calmly when a situation escalates. Hearthstone screens for that experience before a clinician is ever offered a behavioral health placement, so the professional who walks onto your unit already understands the realities of the work.

We support inpatient psychiatric hospitals, behavioral health units inside acute hospitals, substance-use and detox programs, crisis stabilization units, and residential treatment centers throughout Harris, Fort Bend, and Montgomery Counties. Whether you need short-notice per diem coverage for a call-out or sustained contract support during a census surge, we place clinicians who are ready for the assignment.

How Behavioral Health Placement Works

Facilities with an active Hearthstone staffing agreement can request behavioral health coverage through a clear, safety-first process.

  • Submit your open shift — unit type, acuity, and skill level needed
  • We match a clinician with relevant behavioral health experience

  • Credentialing packet provided before the shift begins
  • Clinician arrives briefed on your unit protocols and safety plan
  • Timesheet and invoice documented after shift completion

FAQ

Behavioral Health Staffing — Common Questions

Do your clinicians have de-escalation and crisis training?

Yes. For behavioral health placements we prioritize clinicians with documented de-escalation and crisis-prevention training, and we confirm it before matching anyone to a psychiatric or crisis unit. Where a role requires a specific safe-hold or restraint certification, we verify that as well.

What behavioral health settings do you staff?

Hearthstone places clinicians in inpatient psychiatric hospitals, behavioral health units inside acute hospitals, substance-use and detox programs, crisis stabilization units, and residential treatment centers across Greater Houston.

How quickly can you provide behavioral health coverage?

For facilities with an active staffing agreement, our placement target is 36–72 hours from a confirmed request, depending on the role, availability and credentialing. Because behavioral health requires the right experience match, we never compromise screening for speed.

Can you provide one-to-one observation staff?

Yes. We place behavioral health technicians and CNAs for one-to-one and continuous observation, milieu support, and daily living assistance — common needs on units managing high-acuity or high-risk patients.

Are placements per diem only, or can we get ongoing coverage?

Both. We offer per diem shift coverage as well as ongoing and contract staffing for sustained needs. If you anticipate weeks or months of coverage, a contract agreement adds stability and consistency for your unit.
