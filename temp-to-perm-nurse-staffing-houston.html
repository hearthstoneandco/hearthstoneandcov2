Hire with more than an interview

A résumé and two interviews only tell you so much. Temp-to-perm lets you watch a nurse or CNA handle your census, your charting and your team before you commit to a permanent role. For clinicians, it is a way to see a facility from the inside before accepting an offer.

We recruit locally across Harris, Fort Bend and Montgomery Counties, so we can often match clinicians who live near your facility and want a stable schedule.

What you get

Credentialed before day one

License verification, background and drug screening, and OIG/SAM exclusion checks are completed before a clinician’s first shift.

W-2 clinicians

Our clinicians are W-2 employees of Hearthstone & Co., paid weekly, so payroll administration for the assignment stays with us.

A real timeline

We aim to fill roles within 36–72 hours after the request is confirmed, and we tell you up front if a role needs longer.

Direct access

You work directly with our founder and team, with no ticket queue.

When temp-to-perm fits

It works best for roles that are hard to hire quickly: night-shift LVNs and CNAs in skilled nursing, weekend RN coverage, and positions where an open seat is already affecting care. If you only need shift coverage, see per diem staffing. For longer fixed-term needs, see contract and travel coverage.

Conversion terms differ by facility and role, so contact us to discuss conversion for your situation.

Temp-to-Perm Staffing FAQs

What is temp-to-perm nurse staffing?
A temporary placement with the option to convert the clinician to a permanent employee of your facility after you have worked together.
Which roles can be temp-to-perm?
RN, LVN and CNA placements for skilled nursing, assisted living, memory care, clinics and other Houston-area settings.
Can I convert a clinician to a permanent hire?
That is the purpose of the arrangement. Contact us to discuss conversion for your facility.
I am a nurse. Can I find temp-to-perm work through you?
Yes. Apply through Join Our Network and tell us you are interested in a path to permanent work.
