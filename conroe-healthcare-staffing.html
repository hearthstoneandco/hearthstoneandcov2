Healthcare Staffing for Conroe Facilities

Conroe is in Montgomery County, north of Houston along Interstate 45, State Highway 105 and Loop 336, near Lake Conroe. Conroe is the Montgomery County seat and one of the faster-growing cities in the Houston region. Its hospital, rehabilitation, skilled nursing and senior living settings recruit from the same nurse pool as The Woodlands, which makes short-notice coverage harder to find.

Facilities in Conroe typically need RN and LVN coverage for hospital, rehab and skilled nursing units, and CNA coverage for senior living and memory care. Our core is RN staffing, LVN staffing and CNA staffing, on per diem, contract and temp-to-perm terms. Hearthstone also hires and staffs allied health and other medical roles.

How a Conroe Staffing Request Works

  1. Send the request. Role, unit, shift times, start date and any required certifications, through the staffing request form or by phone.
  2. We respond within 24-48 hours to begin reviewing the request and confirm a realistic timeline.
  3. We work to fill the role within 36-72 hours of your initial request. That is a placement target, not a guarantee, and we tell you plainly if your timeline is tighter than we can meet.
  4. A credentialed W-2 clinician arrives. Credentialing is managed by Hearthstone: Checkr and FACIS Level 3 background screening, Quest drug screening, five-step credentialing and monthly OIG and SAM exclusion checks. See compliance and credentialing.
