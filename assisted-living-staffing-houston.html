Staffing Built for Assisted Living Communities

Assisted living runs on consistency. Residents know their caregivers, families notice every new face, and one weekend call-out can stretch a small team thin. Hearthstone & Co. places CNAs, LVNs and RNs to help Houston-area communities cover shifts while they recruit permanent staff.

CNA Coverage

Certified nursing assistants for personal care and daily-living support on day, evening, overnight and weekend shifts, including memory care units.

LVN Coverage

Licensed vocational nurses for communities that need a licensed nurse on the schedule for resident monitoring and medication-related tasks within LVN scope.

RN Coverage

Registered nurses for resident assessment and clinical oversight. Under Texas rules an RN performs the assessments and delegation described below.

Per diem coverage for the shifts that are hardest to fill: overnights, weekends and last-minute call-outs. See per diem nurse staffing.

Texas Assisted Living Staffing Basics

A quick reference for administrators and wellness nurses. Assisted living facilities are licensed by Texas HHSC under 26 Tex. Admin. Code Chapter 553. Rules change, so confirm current requirements with HHSC before relying on this summary. For nursing homes, which fall under a different chapter, see our nursing home staffing page.

Type A and Type B Licenses

Under § 553.5, Type A residents must be able to evacuate without physical help from staff. Type B residents may need staff help to evacuate or attendance during nighttime sleeping hours, but may not be permanently bedfast. Your license type shapes the coverage you need, so mention it in your request.

Orientation and Training

Under § 553.253, the facility must document that staff are competent to provide personal care, with four hours of orientation before any job responsibilities, 16 hours of on-the-job supervision and training for attendants, and six hours of annual education for direct care staff. The rule text does not address agency staff, so confirm with HHSC how it applies to your community.

RN Assessment and Delegation

Under § 553.263, an RN assesses whether a health maintenance activity can be performed by personal care staff without delegation, and medication administration by personal care staff requires proper RN delegation under Board of Nursing rules (22 Tex. Admin. Code Chapter 225). The Texas BON delegation resources state that the RN retains licensure responsibility for assessing and delegating.

Memory Care Staffing

For Alzheimer's-certified facilities, § 553.305 requires sufficient staff to meet residents' needs and, in facilities or units with 17 or more residents, two staff members immediately available when residents are present.

What to Include in Your Staffing Request

The more specific the request, the more accurately we can confirm a timeline. Include:

Role and Shift

RN, LVN or CNA; dates; day, evening, overnight or weekend; one-time, recurring or ongoing.

Setting

Assisted living or memory care, your license type (A or B), and approximate census for the unit.

Your Requirements

Orientation, immunization and documentation requirements, and any facility systems staff will use. We confirm what we can meet before a clinician is scheduled.

Assisted Living Staffing FAQs

What roles do you staff for assisted living communities?
Our core is CNAs, LVNs and RNs. We also hire and staff allied healthcare and other medical roles, so ask us about any role you need and we will confirm what we can cover.
How fast can you fill a call-out?
Our placement target is 36 to 72 hours after your initial request. This is a target, not a guarantee. Actual timing depends on role, shift and specialty, and we confirm a realistic timeline when you share your requirements.
Do you cover overnights, weekends and census changes?
Yes. We offer per diem coverage for call-outs and overnight or weekend gaps, and longer assignments for sustained needs. Tell us the dates and shifts in your request.
How do you screen clinicians?
Screening includes background checks through Checkr and FACIS Level 3, Quest drug screening, and monthly OIG and SAM exclusion checks, with credentialing managed by Hearthstone. See our compliance and credentialing page for more detail.
Are your clinicians W-2 employees?
Yes. Our clinicians are W-2 employees, paid weekly, and are covered by Hearthstone workers' compensation. That is what we mean by zero employment risk for your community on our placements.
Who delegates medication tasks in assisted living in Texas?
Under 26 Tex. Admin. Code section 553.263, medication administration by personal care staff requires an RN to determine it can be delegated and to delegate it properly under Texas Board of Nursing rules (22 Tex. Admin. Code Chapter 225). Confirm current rules with Texas HHSC.
How do I request staffing?
Use the staffing request form, call (713) 938-3989, or email facilities@hearthstoneandco.com. You can also book a 20-minute call. We will confirm next steps and a realistic timeline.
