About Hearthstone & Co.

Hearthstone & Co. Workforce Solutions is a healthcare staffing company based in Katy, Texas, serving Greater Houston. We place credentialed RNs, LVNs and CNAs with skilled nursing facilities, assisted living communities, hospitals and clinics, and we employ our clinicians as W-2 employees.

We are an SBA-certified Veteran-Owned (VOSB) and Woman-Owned (WOSB) small business and a NaVOBA-certified Veteran’s Business Enterprise. Our founder, Renaye Mullings, is a U.S. Army veteran. Founded in 2026, we are a growing company where your work has real influence on how we operate and serve our clients.

The Role

The Account Executive turns conversations into contracts. You meet with Directors of Nursing, administrators, practice managers and other decision-makers at skilled nursing facilities, assisted living communities, hospitals and clinics, understand their staffing needs, and guide them to a signed agreement with Hearthstone & Co.

You take the meetings our outreach team books, own the deal from discovery to close, and hand each new client to our operations team so the first shifts go smoothly.

What Your Day-to-Day Looks Like

Your working hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Typical responsibilities include:

  • Prepare for each meeting by researching the facility, its decision-makers, its census and staffing situation, and any agency it uses today.
  • Lead discovery calls and video meetings with decision-makers to uncover staffing gaps, shift needs, roles required and what has gone wrong with other agencies.
  • Present how Hearthstone & Co. solves their problem, including our credentialing process, W-2 clinician model and Houston focus, in clear, honest terms.
  • Prepare and send proposals and service agreements, and work with the Founder to finalize terms.
  • Follow up persistently and professionally with every open opportunity, and handle objections about price, timing, other vendors and trust.
  • Negotiate and close: get contracts signed and facility onboarding paperwork started.
  • Hand off each new client to the Clinician Coordinator and Recruiting team with a complete brief, and check in after the first shifts.
  • Keep every deal, note, next step and forecast current in the CRM.

Each week you will also:

  • Review your pipeline with the Director of Sales and commit to a closing forecast.
  • Work with the BDR/SDR team on target accounts and meeting quality.
  • Share what you hear in the market about competitors, rates and facility needs with leadership.

What We’re Looking For

  • 2+ years of B2B sales or account executive experience with a track record of closing deals.
  • Confident presenting to senior decision-makers and comfortable asking for the signature.
  • Skilled at discovery: you ask good questions and listen.
  • Self-managed and organized, with disciplined CRM habits and pipeline forecasting.
  • Willing to learn healthcare staffing and how facilities buy it.

Nice to have:

  • Healthcare, staffing or other B2B services sales experience.
  • An existing network of facility decision-makers in Greater Houston.
  • Military or veteran background.

Compensation

This is a full-time, salaried W-2 position with a $60,000 base salary and $100,000 on-target earnings (OTE), which includes commission. You are paid every other week (biweekly). Overtime is not expected in this role, and any overtime must be approved in advance.
