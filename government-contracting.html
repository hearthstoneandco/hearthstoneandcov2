Federal Partnership FAQs

Is Hearthstone a certified small business?
Yes. Hearthstone & Co. Workforce Solutions LLC is SBA-certified as both a Veteran-Owned Small Business (VOSB) and a Woman-Owned Small Business (WOSB), verifiable through SBA VetCert and SBA Certify. Our SAM.gov registration is active: UEI SVAZQG49VUH7, CAGE 1ZPE9.
Do you compete for prime contracts?
Our federal focus is subcontracting and teaming. We serve as a regional recruiting partner underneath established primes — extending their Texas delivery capacity rather than competing for their seats. That makes us a conflict-free addition to any team.
How does partnering with Hearthstone help a prime contractor?
Two ways. Operationally, we recruit, credential, and W-2 employ clinicians against your Texas task-order requirements so requisitions fill faster. Contractually, our certified VOSB and WOSB dollars count toward the small-business subcontracting goals your subcontracting plan is measured on.
What engagement structures do you support?
Three: a subcontract for defined labor categories under your task order, a teaming agreement pre-positioned for upcoming Texas requirements, or a recruiting-services agreement for per-placement sourcing support.
Which personnel categories do you staff?
Registered Nurses (RN), Licensed Vocational Nurses (LVN/LPN), Certified Nursing Assistants (CNA), and medical support personnel — recruited from the Texas labor market, credentialed through our five-step compliance process, and employed W-2 by Hearthstone.
