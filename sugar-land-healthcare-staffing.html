When a call-out or a vacancy leaves a unit short, you need a staffing partner who can send a vetted clinician quickly, not a call center that sends a résumé. Sugar Land Healthcare Staffing FAQs

Do you staff facilities in Sugar Land?
Yes. We place RNs, LVNs, and CNAs with skilled nursing facilities, assisted living and memory care communities, clinics, and other healthcare settings in Sugar Land and across Fort Bend County. We are based in Katy, so Sugar Land is part of our core service area.
Which Fort Bend communities do you cover?
Sugar Land, Missouri City, Stafford, Richmond, Rosenberg, and Fulshear, along with the rest of Fort Bend County and the wider Greater Houston area across Harris and Montgomery Counties.
How quickly can a Sugar Land facility get a clinician?
Every open shift carries a 24–72 hour placement guarantee. Role-based targets are CNA 12–24 hours, LVN 18–36 hours, RN 24–48 hours, and RN specialty 36–72 hours, and we confirm a realistic timeline for your request up front. We respond to new requests within one business day.
Are your clinicians W-2 employees?
Yes. Every Hearthstone clinician is a W-2 employee of our agency, so your facility is not taking on payroll, tax, or classification paperwork for the clinician.
How do we get started?
Submit a staffing request online or call (713) 364-5058. Tell us the role, shift, and start date and we will confirm requirements and match credentialed clinicians.

Looking for a specific role? See our RN staffing, LVN staffing, and CNA staffing pages, or learn about our compliance and credentialing process.
