Texas LVN Scope of Practice and Exclusion Screening: What Facilities Should Know

Directed practice. The Texas Board of Nursing describes the LVN role as providing safe, compassionate, focused nursing care to assigned patients with predictable health care needs, and states that the LVN scope of practice is a directed scope that requires appropriate supervision. Plan unit coverage so each LVN has the supervision the Board requires, and confirm each nurse's license status before a shift.

Exclusion checks. The HHS Office of Inspector General states that anyone who hires an individual or entity on its List of Excluded Individuals/Entities may be subject to civil monetary penalties, that no payment will be made for items or services furnished, ordered or prescribed by an excluded person, and that health care entities should routinely check the list for new hires and current employees. Our process includes monthly OIG and SAM exclusion checks.

LVN Staffing FAQ

How fast can Hearthstone & Co. place an LVN in Houston?

We respond to every facility staffing request within 24-48 hours to begin reviewing it, and we aim to fill the role within 36-72 hours after the initial request. The 36-72 hour figure is a placement target, not a guarantee, and timing depends on the shift, location and credentials required.

Are your LVNs W-2 employees?

Yes. Hearthstone & Co. uses a W-2 employment model, and W-2 clinicians are covered by Hearthstone workers' compensation. See why W-2 staffing matters for facilities.

How do you screen LVNs before placement?

Background screening runs through Checkr and FACIS Level 3, with Quest drug screening, a five-step credentialing process managed by Hearthstone, and monthly OIG and SAM exclusion checks.

What does an LVN do in a Texas nursing home or assisted living community?

The Texas Board of Nursing describes LVN practice as providing safe, compassionate, focused nursing care to assigned patients with predictable health care needs, under appropriate supervision. Duties in a given facility depend on its policies, the nurse's competencies and the Board's rules. Tell us the unit and expectations when you request staffing.

Does an LVN need to be supervised?

According to the Texas Board of Nursing, the LVN scope of practice is a directed scope of practice that requires appropriate supervision, for example by a registered nurse, advanced practice registered nurse, physician assistant or physician. Facilities should confirm their supervision arrangements for each shift.

What LVN staffing models do you offer?

We staff per diem and contract LVN shifts. Tell us the unit, shift pattern and start date when you request staffing and we will confirm what we can cover.

What happens if a facility employs someone on the OIG exclusion list?

HHS-OIG states that anyone who hires an individual or entity on its List of Excluded Individuals/Entities (LEIE) may be subject to civil monetary penalties, and that no payment will be made for items or services furnished, ordered or prescribed by an excluded person. OIG advises routinely checking the LEIE for new hires and current employees. Our process includes monthly OIG and SAM exclusion checks.

Do you only staff LVNs?

No. RN, LVN and CNA staffing is our core, and we also hire and staff allied healthcare and other types of medical staffing. See our RN staffing, CNA staffing and allied health staffing pages.

What are your LVN rates?

We offer competitive rates for per diem and contract LVNs. Contact us for a written quote for your facility.
